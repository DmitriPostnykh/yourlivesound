About Me

More than 25 years ago, I chose sound engineering as my profession. Since then I have worked with musicians, artists, production centers, theaters and recording studios, building experience across live, studio and system work.

Great sound brings together physics, music, psychoacoustics, electronics, engineering and modern technology. It also takes careful listening, patience, clear communication and a willingness to experiment. My goal is simple: create an atmosphere that does not leave the listener indifferent.
